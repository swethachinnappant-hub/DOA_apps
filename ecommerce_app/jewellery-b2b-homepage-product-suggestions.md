# Jewellery B2B Homepage Product Suggestions

## 1. Recommended Homepage Product Sections

### 01. Best Sellers
- Show products that retailers frequently purchase or that have strong sales performance.
- Feature gold necklaces, bangles, earrings, and pendants.
- Add a Best Seller badge.
- Display wholesale price and minimum order quantity (MOQ).
- Provide a Quick Add to Cart action.

### 02. New Arrivals
- Highlight recently added designs so retailers can discover fresh stock.
- Add a New Arrival badge.
- Display the recently added date where useful.
- Show the product SKU.
- Include a View All action.

### 03. Trending Jewellery
- Showcase popular styles and current customer favourites.
- Use a Trending Now badge where appropriate.
- Feature popular designs and occasion-based products.
- Use dynamic product recommendations based on real data.

### 04. Bridal & Festive Collection
- Feature designs for weddings, festivals, and special occasions.
- Include bridal necklaces, temple jewellery, matching earrings and bangles, and complete jewellery sets.

### 05. Wholesale Deals
- Help buyers find products within their purchasing budget.
- Create price collections such as Under ₹500 and Under ₹1,500.
- Highlight bulk purchase offers.
- Display quantity-based pricing where available.

### 06. Complete the Look
- Recommend matching products that retailers can purchase together.
- Examples:
  - Necklace + earrings
  - Chain + pendant
  - Bangle + ring
  - Matching jewellery sets

## 3. Product Card Requirements

Each product card should show the information a wholesale buyer needs at a glance.

- High-quality product image
- Product name
- Wholesale price
- SKU or product code
- Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
- Wishlist icon
- Quick-add button
- Stock availability

## 4. Recommended Homepage Order

1. Header and search
2. Promotional hero banner
3. Shop by Category
4. Best Sellers
5. New Arrivals
6. Shop by Metal
7. Trending Jewellery
8. Wholesale Deals by Price
9. Bridal & Festive Collection
10. Featured Collections
11. Recently Viewed
12. Bottom Navigation

This order gives buyers quick access to popular products first, then helps them explore by material, budget, and collection.

## 5. Top Five Priorities

1. Add a **Best Sellers** product carousel.
2. Add **New Arrivals** with clear product cards.
3. Display **wholesale price, SKU, and MOQ**.
4. Improve the **Shop by Metal** filters.
5. Add **Wholesale Deals by Price**.

## Final Recommendation

Make Best Sellers, New Arrivals, and Wholesale Deals the three main product sections. They will give the Jewellery B2B homepage a more complete marketplace feel while helping retailers discover products and place bulk orders efficiently.
