AI Powered Accounting ERP & CRM Platform

Software Requirement Document (Version 1.0)

1. Project Vision

Develop a cloud-based Web Application + Android + iOS Mobile Application that completely digitalizes the accounting process of clients and reduces manual data entry by more than 90%.

The platform should automate GST compliance, accounting, document management, CRM, communication, and client collaboration using Artificial Intelligence (AI).

⸻

2. Objectives

Current Problems

	•	Manual collection of invoices
	•	Manual accounting entries
	•	Manual GST preparation
	•	Time-consuming reconciliation
	•	Delayed filing
	•	Heavy manpower requirement
	•	Missing documents
	•	Difficulty during GST Audit & Department Scrutiny

Proposed Solution

One single platform where

	•	Client uploads invoices
	•	AI reads invoices automatically
	•	Accounting entries are created automatically
	•	Accountant verifies
	•	GST Returns are generated
	•	Tally sync happens
	•	Records remain permanently stored
